RecMix Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 21, 2026

RecMix is a screen recording and video editing app. This policy explains what information the app uses and how it is handled.

Information RecMix Uses

RecMix may access or create the following information only when needed for app features:

  • Screen recordings created by the user.
  • Microphone audio when the user enables microphone recording.
  • Device/internal audio when the user enables internal audio capture and Android allows it.
  • Camera preview/video overlay when the user enables the optional front camera overlay.
  • Drawing input created by the user while recording.
  • Video files saved, edited, shared, exported, or deleted by the user.
  • Local app settings, such as recording quality, audio source, countdown, and related preferences.
  • Basic crash or diagnostic files stored locally on the device to help identify app problems.

How Information Is Used

RecMix uses this information to provide screen recording, camera overlay, audio capture, drawing, editing, export, sharing, and file management features. User recordings are stored locally on the device unless the user chooses to share or export them through Android sharing or gallery features.

Accounts and Login

RecMix does not require an account, login, username, password, or user profile.

Advertising

RecMix displays ads using Google AdMob. Google may collect device identifiers, advertising identifiers, usage data, diagnostics, and other information according to Google's advertising and privacy policies. RecMix does not sell personal information.

Data Sharing

RecMix does not run its own backend service for user accounts or cloud storage. Recordings and exported videos remain on the device unless the user chooses to share them. Advertising and related diagnostics may be processed by Google AdMob as described in Google's policies.

Data Security

Recordings and app preferences are stored locally on the user's device. Data handled by third-party services such as Google AdMob is transmitted using their secure service connections.

Deleting Data

Users can delete recordings inside RecMix or from their device storage. Users can clear app data or uninstall RecMix to remove local app settings and locally stored diagnostic files.

Children

RecMix is not directed to children. The intended audience is users age 13 and older.

Health, Financial, and Government Use

RecMix is not a health app, financial app, or government app. It does not provide health services, financial services, government services, or related regulated features.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when RecMix changes its features or data practices. The updated date at the top of this page will reflect the latest version.

Contact

For privacy questions about RecMix, contact the app publisher through the contact information listed on the Google Play Store page.
